## 2. What Nett does

Nett works out UK take-home pay, tax, National Insurance, student loan and pension figures from HMRC's published rules for the tax year you select and the details you enter. Its figures are estimates. They aren't financial, tax, legal or investment advice, and they don't know everything about your circumstances. Before a decision that matters, check with HMRC or a qualified adviser.

## 3. Accuracy

We check Nett's figures against HMRC's published rates and worked examples, and test them before each release. Your payslip can still differ, for example because of how your employer's payroll rounds, a tax code HMRC has changed, or income or deductions Nett doesn't know about. If you think a figure is wrong, email us and we'll look into it.
