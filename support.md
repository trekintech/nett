# Nett: Support

Questions, a figure that looks wrong, or something not working? Email **admin@trekintech.com** and we'll reply within two working days. Please don't attach payslips or other documents with personal details; a description and the figures are enough.

## Common questions

**My take-home doesn't match my payslip.** Check the tax code, pension and student loan in Account › Tax setup match your payslip, then use Summary › Check a payslip: it compares each line and explains any difference. If it still looks wrong, email us the figures.

**How do I restore Nett Pro on a new phone?** Open the paywall from any Pro feature and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

**How do I cancel Nett Pro?** In the app, Account › Nett Pro › Manage, or in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

**How do I get a refund?** Refunds go through Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Email us too if something went wrong and we'll help.

**Where's my data?** On your device. If you switch on iCloud Backup it's also in your own private iCloud account, which we can't see. Details are in the [privacy policy](https://nett.trekintech.com/privacy-policy).

**How do I delete everything?** Account › Start again clears your data. If you used iCloud Backup, also delete Nett's data in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.
